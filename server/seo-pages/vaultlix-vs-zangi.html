Vaultlix vs Zangi

Comparison · Last checked 17 September 2026 against vaultlix.com and zangi.com
The short version: both give you a system-generated number instead of asking for your phone number, and both need the other person to use the same app. Zangi is a full messenger with groups, large group calls and an optional paid plan, available as an iPhone, Android and Huawei app. Vaultlix is built only for one-to-one conversations that start with an accepted request, works in the browser today, and lets you restore your account on another device.

Signing up and your number

Vaultlix: choose the username people will see and Vaultlix gives you a permanent number. You sign in with your Vaultlix number and password, and keep a recovery code. Losing both your device and your recovery code permanently loses your Vaultlix number.

Zangi: Zangi generates a 10-digit number during registration without asking for a phone number, email or SIM details. Its FAQ says it works on a one-account, one-device basis and that password reset isn't possible because it doesn't collect identity information.

Privacy and encryption

Vaultlix: messages are end-to-end encrypted with keys held by the people in the conversation, and call media is encrypted between participant devices. Vaultlix stores encrypted message content for delivery; the Privacy Policy lists exactly what exists on its servers.

Zangi: Zangi describes end-to-end encryption and says your data lives only on your device, with files passed through a temporary repository that clears after download.

Neither company's claims have been independently verified here. We didn't find a published independent security audit on either website.

Who can contact you

Vaultlix: there is no contact search or suggestions. Someone needs your exact number to send a request, and a conversation appears only after you accept. You can erase a conversation for both people, or report and block.

Zangi: there is no profile lookup; people need your full Zangi number to find you.

Choose Zangi if…

  • You need group chats or group calls.
  • You want a native app on iPhone, Android or Huawei right now.
  • You prefer an app that says it keeps your data only on your device.

Choose Vaultlix if…

  • You want every new conversation to start only after you accept a request.
  • You want to use it in a web browser without installing an app.
  • You want to restore your account on another device with your password and recovery code.
  • You want safety controls like an independent App Lock, Quick Lock, Emergency Exit and erase-for-both.
