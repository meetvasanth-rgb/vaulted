Vaultlix vs Zangi

Comparison · Last checked 17 September 2026 against vaultlix.com and zangi.com
The short version: both give you a system-generated number instead of your phone number. Vaultlix is built for private one-to-one conversations that start only when you accept, works instantly in any browser, and lets you recover your account with a recovery code if you forget your password. Zangi is a broader messenger with groups and large group calls; its FAQ says password reset isn't possible.

Why people choose Vaultlix

  • You can get back in. Forget your password and you can still recover your account with your Vaultlix number and recovery code. Zangi's FAQ says password reset isn't possible because it doesn't collect identity information.
  • Nobody reaches you without your OK. Having your number isn't enough: every conversation starts with a request you accept.
  • Private one-to-one by design. Every conversation is between two people who both agreed to connect.
  • Safety controls for everyone. Independent App Lock, Quick Lock, Emergency Exit and erase-for-both are built into every Vaultlix account.
  • Works instantly in any browser. No download or app store needed. Open vaultlix.com and create your number.

Signing up and your number

Vaultlix: choose the username people will see and Vaultlix gives you a permanent number. You sign in with your Vaultlix number and password, and keep a recovery code for account recovery. Keep the recovery code safe: losing both your device and your recovery code permanently loses your Vaultlix number.

Zangi: Zangi generates a 10-digit number during registration without asking for a phone number, email or SIM details. Its FAQ says it works on a one-account, one-device basis and that password reset isn't possible because it doesn't collect identity information.

Privacy and encryption

Vaultlix: messages are end-to-end encrypted with keys held by the people in the conversation, and call media is encrypted between participant devices. Vaultlix documents exactly what exists on its servers in its Privacy Policy, including encrypted message content kept for delivery and encrypted files kept in private storage.

Zangi: Zangi describes end-to-end encryption and says your data lives only on your device. It says files are split into encrypted parts and sent through a temporary repository that clears once the recipient downloads them.

Neither company's claims have been independently verified here, and we didn't find a published independent security audit on either website.

Where you can use it

Vaultlix: use it today in any browser at vaultlix.com. The iPhone app is in App Store review and Android is in limited testing. You sign in with the same Vaultlix number and password wherever you use it. See Get Vaultlix for current status.

Zangi: apps for iPhone, Android and Huawei AppGallery.

Choose Vaultlix if…

  • You want to be able to recover your account if you forget your password.
  • You want every new conversation to start only after you accept a request.
  • You want safety controls like App Lock, Quick Lock, Emergency Exit and erase-for-both built in.
  • You want to start right away in a browser, without installing anything.

Choose Zangi if…

  • You need group chats or group calls.
  • You want a native iPhone, Android or Huawei app right now.
  • You prefer an app that says it keeps your data only on your device, and you're comfortable that password reset isn't possible.

Common questions

Is Vaultlix a Zangi alternative?

Yes, if you want a private number for one-to-one conversations. Like Zangi, Vaultlix doesn't need your phone number or email. With Vaultlix, every conversation starts with a request you accept, and you can recover your account with a recovery code if you forget your password.

Can I recover my Vaultlix account if I forget my password?

Yes. Use Recover account with your Vaultlix number and recovery code, then choose a new password. Keep your recovery code somewhere outside your device.

Does Vaultlix support group chats?

No. Vaultlix is built only for one-to-one conversations. If you need groups, Zangi or another group messenger will suit you better.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Vaultlix works in your web browser at vaultlix.com.
