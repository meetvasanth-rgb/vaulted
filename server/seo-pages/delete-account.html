Delete your Vaultlix account and data

Vaultlix · Updated 17 September 2026
You can delete your account in Vaultlix or request help by email. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

Delete from the app

  1. Sign in to the Vaultlix account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete Account.
  3. Read the deletion notice and complete the confirmation.

This is permanent. Wait for the process to finish; if an error appears, retry or contact us below.

Request deletion without the app

Email privacy@vaultlix.com with the subject “Vaultlix account deletion request”. Include your Vaultlix Private Number and say that you want your account and associated data deleted. You do not need to reinstall the app to send a request.

We will reply with the steps needed to verify ownership and confirm the outcome. A Private Number alone is not proof of ownership. Never email your password, recovery code or encryption keys. Vaultlix does not require an email address at signup, so the email you write from is not automatically linked to your account.

What is deleted

Account deletion removes your active account record, display name, profile photo, authentication records, notification registrations and encrypted account backup, and invalidates your sign-in sessions. The in-app deletion flow also closes the conversations it retrieves from your encrypted account backup, removes their server-held content, and clears Vaultlix data on the device used for deletion.

For email requests, we will explain any additional steps needed to remove conversation data held under separate encrypted access credentials. Copies another person has independently saved, downloaded or captured cannot be erased by Vaultlix.

Data that may be retained

  • A retired-number record is retained to prevent your former Private Number from being assigned to someone else. It records the number, retirement reason and date.
  • Safety reports may remain while unresolved. Resolved reports and review notes are removed 90 days after their last review update.
  • Infrastructure backups may retain copies until the backup expires or is deleted; deletion does not instantly erase existing backups.

See the Vaultlix Privacy Policy for further information, or ask privacy@vaultlix.com about a specific deletion request.
